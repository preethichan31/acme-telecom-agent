No Service / No Signal
When a customer reports no service or no signal, first confirm the customer's location and check for known outages in their area. Ask the customer to power cycle their device (turn off and on). If the issue persists, have the customer toggle Airplane Mode on and off. Check if the SIM card is properly seated by asking the customer to remove and reinsert it. Verify the account is in good standing and not suspended for non-payment. If all steps fail and there is no known outage, initiate a line reset. If the issue continues after a line reset, escalate the ticket to Tier 2 Network Engineering.

Dropped Calls / Poor Call Quality
For dropped calls or poor call quality, ask the customer to identify the locations where drops occur. Check for known cell tower outages or maintenance in those areas. Have the customer disable Wi-Fi calling and test again, or enable it if it is disabled. Verify the customer's device software is up to date. Check if the issue is specific to certain numbers — if so, it may be a routing issue requiring escalation. If dropped calls occur in a location where coverage should be strong, collect the timestamps of recent drops and escalate to Tier 2 Network Engineering with high priority. If the issue is widespread or consistently affects service at the customer's home, schedule a technician visit to assess signal strength.

Slow or No Mobile Data
For slow or no mobile data, first check if the customer has exceeded their monthly data allowance — if so, explain the throttling policy and offer to add a data boost. Verify the customer's APN settings are correct for their device. Ask the customer to toggle mobile data off and on. Check for network congestion in the customer's area using the network status portal. If on a 5G-capable device, verify 5G is enabled in device settings. Check for pending software updates. If speeds remain below 1 Mbps and the data allowance has not been exceeded, collect speed test results and escalate to Tier 2 with the data collected.

Text Messages Not Sending or Receiving
For SMS/MMS issues, verify the customer's messaging app settings and ensure they are not blocking the sender. Have the customer clear the messaging app cache. Check if the issue is specific to iMessage versus SMS — advise the customer to disable iMessage if they recently switched from iOS to Android. Verify messaging services are enabled on the account. If MMS is failing, check APN settings. Perform a line reset if basic SMS is failing. If the issue persists after a line reset, escalate to Tier 2.

Voicemail Issues
For voicemail access problems, verify voicemail is provisioned on the account. Walk the customer through accessing voicemail by dialing *86. If voicemail is full, advise the customer to delete old messages. If the customer cannot receive voicemail notifications, check notification settings on their device. To reset a voicemail PIN, the customer can dial 611 from their Acme Telecom line. If voicemail provisioning appears corrupted, perform a line reset and allow 15 minutes for voicemail to re-provision. If still failing, escalate to Tier 2 with account details.

Billing Disputes and Unexpected Charges
For billing disputes, pull up the customer's last three billing statements. Explain each line item clearly. Common unexpected charges include: international roaming (advise customer to add a Travel Pass before traveling), data overage fees (offer a data boost or plan upgrade), premium SMS charges from third-party subscriptions (remove and block if unauthorized), and activation fees for new lines. If a charge appears to be in error, a one-time courtesy credit of up to $25 can be issued without escalation. For larger credits or recurring billing errors, escalate the ticket to the Billing Escalations team with high priority.

Port and Number Transfer Issues
For number porting issues, verify the account number and PIN match exactly what the losing carrier has on file. Ensure the customer's current account is not past due. Check the porting status in the system. Wireless number ports typically complete in 2–4 hours; landline ports take 2–5 business days. If a wireless port has been pending more than 4 hours or a landline port more than 5 days, escalate to the Port Center with the port-in order number.

5G and eSIM Troubleshooting
If a customer reports no 5G signal on a 5G-capable plan and device: confirm 5G coverage at their location using the coverage map; verify the device's 5G toggle is enabled (Settings > Cellular > Voice & Data > 5G Auto); restart the device to force re-registration on the network. If 5G still does not appear, reset network settings and re-add the APN profile. For eSIM activation issues: confirm the activation QR code has not already been consumed (each code is single-use); ensure the device has a stable Wi-Fi connection during activation; if activation stalls past 10 minutes, remove the pending eSIM profile and rescan a freshly issued QR code from the account portal. eSIM transfers between devices require deactivating the profile on the old device first.
