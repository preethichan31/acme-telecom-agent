Acme Telecom Gateway — Model AGW-3000
The AGW-3000 is Acme Telecom's standard home internet gateway. Indicator lights: solid blue means operational; blinking blue means connecting; solid red means no signal; blinking red means device fault. To perform a soft restart, press and hold the power button for 5 seconds. To perform a factory reset, press and hold the reset pinhole button on the back for 15 seconds until all lights flash — this will erase all custom Wi-Fi settings. Default Wi-Fi credentials are printed on the label on the bottom of the device. The AGW-3000 supports simultaneous 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. If only one band is visible, log into the admin portal at 192.168.1.1 (default credentials: admin/admin) to verify both bands are enabled.

Acme Telecom Fiber Modem — Model AFM-500
The AFM-500 is used for Acme Telecom fiber internet service. It features four Ethernet ports, two phone jacks for digital voice service, and a USB port. Indicator lights: ONT light solid green means the fiber connection is operational; ONT blinking means it is syncing with the fiber node. If the ONT light is red, there may be a physical fiber cut or ONT equipment failure — a technician visit must be scheduled immediately. To restart the AFM-500, unplug the power adapter, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. Do not factory reset the AFM-500 without technician guidance, as it requires remote re-provisioning.

SIM Card Troubleshooting
Acme Telecom SIM cards are available in nano, micro, and full-size formats. If a customer reports "SIM not detected" or "No SIM card": have the customer power off the device, remove the SIM tray using an ejector tool or a straightened paperclip, and inspect the SIM for damage or debris. Reinsert the SIM firmly and power the device back on. If the SIM appears damaged, a replacement SIM can be ordered online (arrives in 1–2 business days) or picked up at any Acme Telecom retail store. eSIM activation requires the customer to use the Acme Telecom app and scan the QR code sent to their email. If eSIM provisioning fails, a line reset must be performed from the support portal.

APN Settings — Manual Configuration
If a customer needs to manually configure APN settings for mobile data: navigate to Settings > Mobile Data > APN Settings on Android, or Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data Network on iOS. Standard LTE/5G data APN: name = Acme Telecom, APN = acmemobile, username = blank, password = blank, MCC = 310, MNC = 410, APN protocol = IPv4/IPv6. For MMS: APN = acmemms, MMSC = http://mms.acmetelecom.com, MMS proxy = 66.209.11.33:80, MMS max message size = 1048576. After saving, toggle Airplane Mode off and on to apply settings. iOS users may need to reset network settings if the APN field is not editable (Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Network Settings — note this also clears saved Wi-Fi passwords).

Hotspot Device — Model AHS-Pro
The AHS-Pro is Acme Telecom's portable Wi-Fi hotspot. Battery life is approximately 12 hours. The device supports up to 15 simultaneous Wi-Fi connections. To power on, press and hold the power button for 3 seconds. The Wi-Fi name and password are displayed on the device screen. To check data usage, press the info button or visit acmetelecom.com/myaccount. If the device cannot connect to the network, verify the SIM is properly inserted and perform a soft reset: power off, remove the SIM, reinsert it, then power on. If still no service, perform a factory reset by holding the reset button for 10 seconds. The device must then be re-activated by calling Acme Telecom support at (800) 555-0101.

Device Compatibility and BYOD
Acme Telecom's network supports GSM and LTE Bands 2, 4, 5, 12, 17, 66, and 71, as well as 5G Sub-6 bands n41 and n71, and mmWave in select urban areas. To check if a customer's device is compatible, ask for the IMEI number and verify it using the BYOD compatibility checker at acmetelecom.com/byod. Unlocked devices from major manufacturers including Apple, Samsung, and Google Pixel are generally compatible. Devices purchased through another carrier may need to be unlocked first — the customer should contact their previous carrier for an unlock code or procedure. After confirming compatibility, the customer activates the device using a new Acme Telecom SIM or via eSIM through the app.
