Service Plans
Acme Telecom offers four personal wireless plans. Basic ($35/month): 5 GB high-speed data, unlimited talk and text. Standard ($55/month): 25 GB high-speed data, unlimited talk and text, mobile hotspot included. Premium Unlimited ($75/month): unlimited data with no throttling below 3 Mbps even during congestion, 50 GB hotspot data, HD streaming. Premium Plus ($90/month): all Premium Unlimited features plus international calling to 30 countries, 100 GB hotspot data, priority network access during congestion, and same-day technical support callback. Family plans include a $10/month discount per additional line (up to 5 lines). All plans include Wi-Fi calling, visual voicemail, and spam call filtering. Upgrades to a higher plan take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Data Policy and Throttling
On Basic and Standard plans, once a customer's monthly high-speed data is exhausted, data speed is reduced to 1 Mbps for the remainder of the billing cycle — sufficient for email and basic browsing but not video streaming. Data resets on the billing cycle anniversary date. Customers can purchase a one-time Data Boost ($10 for 5 GB, valid for the current cycle) via the app or by contacting support. On Premium Unlimited, data is never throttled below 3 Mbps. On Premium Plus, data is deprioritized only after 100 GB of use during periods of severe network congestion.

International Roaming
International roaming is available in 200+ countries. Default roaming rates without an add-on are $0.25/minute for calls, $0.20 per SMS, and $10/MB for data. To avoid unexpected charges, customers should add a Travel Pass ($10/day, available in 40+ countries, includes unlimited talk and text plus 0.5 GB data) or an International Data Pack ($40 for 1 GB, valid for 30 days) before traveling. Premium Plus customers have calling included to 30 countries and can use their full domestic data allowance in Canada and Mexico. Roaming must be enabled in account settings or by calling support before it will work. Roaming charges cannot be retroactively removed, but courtesy credits may be issued for first-time incidents.

Device Upgrades and Trade-ins
Customers are eligible for device upgrades after 12 months of service. Customers on an Equipment Installment Plan (EIP) must pay off the remaining device balance or trade in the device to upgrade. Trade-in values are assessed at acmetelecom.com/trade-in and credited to the account within 2 billing cycles. Screen cracks reduce trade-in value by $50; significant damage may disqualify the device entirely. New devices can be ordered online, in-store, or by calling support. Standard shipping takes 2–3 business days. Customers have a 14-day return window for new devices.

Early Termination and Cancellation
Month-to-month customers can cancel at any time with no fee. Customers on an Equipment Installment Plan (EIP) remain responsible for the remaining device balance. Customers on legacy 2-year contracts are subject to an Early Termination Fee of $175 minus $10 for each completed month of service. To cancel service, customers must call (800) 555-0101 — cancellations cannot be processed online for security reasons. Porting a number to another carrier automatically cancels the Acme Telecom line once the port completes. Final bills are issued at the end of the current billing cycle and include any remaining prorated charges.

Account Security and PIN
Every Acme Telecom account is protected by a 4–6 digit account PIN used to verify identity when contacting support or making account changes. To reset the PIN, the customer can use the Acme Telecom app under Account > Security, or visit a retail store with a government-issued photo ID. Acme Telecom agents will never ask for the full account PIN over the phone — they only verify the last 2 digits. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available and strongly recommended for the online account portal. If a customer suspects account takeover or unauthorized SIM swap, they should call (800) 555-0101 immediately to lock the account.

Contact Information and Support Channels
For general support, call (800) 555-0101 (available 24/7) or use the chat feature in the Acme Telecom app. For network technical issues, call (800) 555-0102. For billing inquiries, call (800) 555-0103 (Monday–Friday 8 AM–8 PM ET). To schedule a technician visit, call (800) 555-0104. Walk-in support is available at any of Acme Telecom's 520 retail locations — visit acmetelecom.com/stores for hours and directions. Premium Plus subscribers have access to priority support with a same-day callback guarantee.


Acme Telecom chat support is provided by an AI virtual assistant, not a human. To meet AI transparency requirements (EU AI Act, Article 50), the assistant must tell customers it is an AI whenever they ask or seem to believe they are talking to a person, and it must never claim to be human or invent a human name. Customers who prefer a person can call general support at (800) 555-0101 (available 24/7) or visit any Acme Telecom retail location.
